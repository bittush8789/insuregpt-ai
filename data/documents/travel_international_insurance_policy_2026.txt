OPERATIVE PREAMBLE
This Policy is a legally binding contract of travel indemnity entered into between Apex Global Assistance & Travel Insurance Corp. (the "Company") and the Travelers named in the Travel Certificate. The Company agrees to indemnify emergency medical costs, transit disruptions, and legal liabilities incurred outside the Republic of India during the insured journey, up to the limits specified in the Schedule of Benefits.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 1 — OVERSEAS EMERGENCY MEDICAL EXPENSES & HOSPITALIZATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Inpatient & Outpatient Medical Care:
    - Indemnifies reasonable, customary, and medically necessary outpatient and inpatient medical treatment expenses incurred abroad due to sudden accidental bodily injury or acute illness contracted during the trip.
    - Coverage Limit: Up to US$ 500,000 (Platinum Plan) or US$ 250,000 (Gold Plan).
    - Scope of Covered Medical Services:
      * Emergency room consultations, physician fees, and ICU accommodation.
      * Diagnostic laboratory investigations, X-rays, MRI scans, and ultrasound tests.
      * Operation theatre fees, surgical appliances, and prescription pharmaceuticals.
    - Pre-Existing Condition Life-Saving Relief Extension: In the event of a sudden, unexpected life-threatening emergency arising from an undisclosed pre-existing condition, the policy covers immediate emergency stabilization treatment up to US$ 2,500.

1.2 Emergency Medical Evacuation:
    - If the insured traveler suffers an acute illness or catastrophic accident in a geographic area where adequate medical infrastructure is lacking, the Company’s 24x7 Global Assistance Network organizes and pays up to US$ 100,000 for medical air evacuation, air ambulance, or specialized ICU transport to the nearest accredited medical center.

1.3 Repatriation of Mortal Remains:
    - In the tragic event of the demise of the Insured during an overseas journey, the Company covers up to US$ 50,000 for the embalming, official consular clearances, and air repatriation of mortal remains back to the home country, or local burial/cremation abroad.

1.4 Emergency Dental Pain Relief:
    - Covers emergency dental procedures administered by a licensed dentist abroad to relieve acute pain caused by injury to natural sound teeth, covered up to US$ 1,000 (subject to a $50 deductible).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2 — TRANSIT DISRUPTIONS, DELAYS & BAGGAGE CLAIMS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Checked Baggage Total Loss:
    - Pays up to US$ 2,000 for the permanent loss or destruction of checked-in baggage in the custody of a scheduled commercial international airline.
    - Single Article Limit: Indemnity capped at 25% of the total baggage sum insured per individual suitcase or luxury item.

2.2 Checked Baggage Delay Allowance:
    - If checked-in baggage is delayed by the common carrier by more than 6 hours from arrival time at destination, the Company reimburses up to US$ 500 for emergency purchase of essential clothing, toiletries, and medications.

2.3 Trip Cancellation & Trip Curtailment:
    - Reimburses non-refundable air tickets, cruise fares, and hotel deposits up to US$ 5,000 if the journey is unavoidably canceled or cut short due to:
      * Death, severe accident, or emergency hospitalization of the traveler, spouse, child, or parent.
      * Quarantine or jury summons ordered by a government authority.
      * Severe natural calamity rendering the destination accommodation uninhabitable.

2.4 Missed Flight Connection:
    - If the traveler misses an onward connecting international flight due to delayed arrival of the incoming connecting scheduled flight by more than 3 hours, the Company reimburses reasonable transit hotel stays and meals up to US$ 500.

2.5 Loss of International Passport & Travel Documents:
    - Covers reasonable expenses up to US$ 500 incurred in obtaining an emergency passport, temporary consular travel certificate, or duplicate entry visas abroad.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 3 — PERSONAL LIABILITY, HIJACK & CONTINGENCY ASSISTANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Third-Party Personal Liability Abroad:
    - Indemnifies the insured traveler up to US$ 200,000 against legal liability for accidental third-party bodily injury or damage to third-party property caused by the insured's personal negligence while in a foreign country.
    - Excludes liability arising from driving rented motorized vehicles (which must be covered by local collision damage waivers - CDW).

3.2 Hijack Distress Allowance:
    - In the event of hijack of a common carrier aircraft in which the insured is traveling for more than 12 continuous hours, the Company pays a distress relief allowance of US$ 250 for every 24 hours of captivity, up to a maximum of US$ 2,500.

3.3 Bail Bond Advance Guarantee:
    - Provides emergency financial cash advance up to US$ 10,000 if the insured is detained or arrested by foreign law enforcement authorities in connection with a non-felonious traffic or bailable misdemeanor offense.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 4 — GENERAL EXCLUSIONS & RESTRICTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 The Company shall not pay for:
    - Traveling Against Medical Advice: Any travel undertaken after a certified physician advised against long-distance travel.
    - Medical Tourism: Journeys undertaken for the primary purpose of receiving medical, surgical, or cosmetic procedures abroad.
    - High-Altitude Expeditions: Mountaineering expeditions exceeding 4,500 meters, extreme sports, or professional racing without specialized adventure endorsements.
    - Sanctioned Territories: Travel into territories subject to United Nations embargoes, active war zones, or restricted military conflict areas.
