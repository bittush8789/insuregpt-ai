PURPOSE & SCOPE
This document prescribes mandatory workflows, evidentiary thresholds, turn-around time (TAT) service level agreements (SLAs), and documentation protocols governing both Cashless and Reimbursement claims for all health, critical illness, and personal accident lines administered under the InsureGPT ecosystem.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 1 — CASHLESS CLAIMS WORKFLOW (EMPANELLED NETWORK HOSPITALS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Planned Hospitalization Workflow:
    - Pre-Authorization Submission: The insured or network hospital insurance desk must submit a completed Pre-Authorization Request Form (Part C) at least 48 hours prior to scheduled patient admission.
    - Initial Authorization SLA: The TPA / Claims Processing Centre must convey either initial approval, query raise, or denial within 1 hour of receiving complete clinical data, complying with the 100% cashless mandate.
    - Interim Approvals: In case of prolonged hospital stays exceeding 5 days, interim bills must be submitted every 72 hours for continuous cashless enhancement.

1.2 Emergency Hospitalization Workflow:
    - Intimation Timeline: Hospitalization must be formally intimated to the Claims Assistance Desk within 24 hours of emergency admission.
    - Expedited Cashless Guarantee: Hospital produces the digital InsureGPT e-Card along with a valid Government Photo ID (Aadhaar, Passport, Voter ID). An initial emergency credit limit is approved within 60 minutes to ensure treatment commences without deposit demands.

1.3 Final Discharge Authorization & Settlement SLA:
    - Discharge Summary & Final Bill Submission: The hospital transmits the itemized final invoice, lab summaries, and signed discharge summary via the digital claims portal.
    - Mandatory 3-Hour Final Authorization: Pursuant to IRDAI Master Circular directions, the insurer must issue final cashless claim settlement approval within 3 hours of receiving the discharge bill from the network hospital.
    - Zero Patient Wait Policy: The patient shall not be detained in the hospital room beyond 3 hours due to pending insurance cashless confirmation.
    - Insured Co-Payment / Non-Medical Liability: The patient settles only non-payable items (Annexure I non-medical charges like registration fees, gloves, personal comfort items, companion meals) and voluntary co-pay deductibles before hospital release.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2 — REIMBURSEMENT CLAIMS WORKFLOW (NON-NETWORK HOSPITALS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Mandatory Claim Intimation Deadlines:
    - Planned Hospitalization in Non-Network Hospital: Written intimation must be submitted at least 48 hours prior to admission.
    - Emergency Admission: Intimation must be lodged within 24 hours of hospital admission or prior to final discharge, whichever is earlier.
    - Required Intimation Details: Policy number, patient name, hospital name & registration number, treating doctor name, tentative clinical diagnosis, and estimated expenditure.

2.2 Dossier Submission Windows:
    - Inpatient Hospitalization Dossier: Must be submitted to the Claims Registry within 30 calendar days from the formal date of hospital discharge.
    - Pre-Hospitalization Bills: Must be submitted alongside the primary inpatient claim dossier within the 30-day window.
    - Post-Hospitalization Bills: Must be submitted within 15 calendar days from the completion of the 90-day post-hospitalization coverage window (maximum 105 days from discharge).
    - Condonation of Delay: Late submissions may be condoned if substantiated with valid reasons (e.g., severe patient incapacitation, natural calamity) with medical director approval.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 3 — COMPREHENSIVE DOCUMENTATION DOSSIER CHECKLIST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every reimbursement submission must satisfy the Eight-Point Verified Dossier Standard:

1. Claim Form:
   - Form Part A: Fully completed, dated, and signed by the insured policyholder or primary claimant.
   - Form Part B: Duly filled, signed, and stamped by the treating physician and the Medical Superintendent of the treating hospital with hospital registration number.

2. Discharge Summary / Card:
   - Original comprehensive discharge summary detailing clinical history, presenting complaints, vital signs at admission, diagnostic findings, medical line of treatment, surgical notes, and patient condition at discharge.

3. Detailed Itemized Final Hospital Invoice:
   - Original final bill bearing the hospital's official printed letterhead, tax registration number, and official "PAID" stamp.
   - Must provide line-item breakups for room charges, ICU charges, operation theatre usage, surgeon fees, anesthetist fees, consultation charges, and nursing care.

4. Original Payment Receipts:
   - Sequential, numbered money receipts corresponding exactly to advance deposits, interim payments, and final balance settlements made to the hospital.
   - Cancelled cheques, credit card swipe slips, or online UPI payment references must accompany receipts.

5. Pharmacy & Consumables Vouchers:
   - Original chemist bills accompanied by corresponding doctor prescription slips specifying drug brand, dosage, and duration of administration.

6. Diagnostic Investigations & Radiological Reports:
   - Original pathology lab reports (blood, urine, tissue biopsy, culture).
   - High-resolution radiological report copies accompanied by physical X-Ray films, CT scan sheets, MRI films, or digital DICOM CDs.

7. Medico-Legal Dossier (Applicable for Accidents / Trauma):
   - Medico-Legal Certificate (MLC) copy authenticated by the casualty medical officer.
   - Police First Information Report (FIR) or Panchnama in case of road traffic accidents (RTA), assault, or industrial injury.
   - Alcohol and Toxicology Screening Report for the driver/patient in vehicular collision cases.

8. KYC Documents & Electronic Bank Mandate:
   - Valid Government Photo ID proof (Aadhaar, Passport, Voter ID) and PAN Card of the claimant.
   - Original cancelled cheque showing printed name of the account holder, account number, and bank IFSC code for direct NEFT/RTGS fund settlement.

SECTION 4 — NON-PAYABLE CONSUMABLES & EXPENSES GUIDELINES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In accordance with IRDAI Standardization Guidelines, the following items are non-payable under standard hospitalization policies unless a dedicated "Consumables Add-On" rider is attached:
- List I: Personal comfort items (baby food, mineral water, tissue paper, toothbrushes, hand sanitizers, slippers, thermometers, luxury toiletries, guest meals).
- List II: Administrative and documentation charges (admission fee, registration card charge, medical records photocopying, discharge card compilation fees, courier charges).
- List III: Miscellaneous medical disposables (aprons, sterile gloves beyond standard surgical bundles, face shields, PPE kits exceeding standard ICU allocation, surgical caps).
- List IV: External supporting equipment (crutches, braces, lumbar belts, cervical collars) unless surgically implanted into the patient's skeletal system.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 5 — FRAUD DETECTION, INVESTIGATION (SIU) & AUDIT TRIGGERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 Special Investigation Unit (SIU) Escalation Triggers:
    - Non-network hospitals with high historical claim frequency or inflated average ticket sizes.
    - Backdated admissions or bills showing overlapping dates across multiple claims.
    - Claims submitted for routine medical management with identical symptom profiles (e.g., clusters of viral fever/gastroenteritis) lacking corresponding physical signs.
    - Discrepancies between pathological lab findings and prescribed pharmaceutical regimens.
    - Hospital facilities operating with less than 10 inpatient beds or lacking a fully equipped operation theatre and qualified round-the-clock medical staff.

5.2 Action on Proven Fraudulent Claims:
    - Instant claim repudiation with forfeiture of premiums paid under Section 45 of the Insurance Act 1938.
    - Cancellation of active policy coverage and reporting of delinquent medical facilities to the National Insurance Fraud Bureau (NIFB) and local law enforcement.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 6 — ADJUDICATION TIMELINES, SETTLEMENT SLAs & ESCALATION MATRIX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.1 Operational SLAs:
    - Claim Dossier Registration & SMS Acknowledgement: Within 24 hours of receipt.
    - Deficiency Intimation Letter: Dispatched within 3 business days of dossier receipt if any document is missing or ambiguous. The insured is allowed 30 days to furnish deficiencies.
    - Final Claim Settlement or Repudiation: Processed within 15 business days of receiving the final complete set of required documentation.
    - Interest on Delayed Claims: As per IRDAI regulations, if settlement is delayed beyond 30 days without valid justification, the insurer pays bank interest at 2.0% above the prevailing bank rate.

6.2 Grievance Redressal & Ombudsman Matrix:
    - Tier 1 (Internal): Claims Grievance Officer (grievance@insuregpt.internal) — Mandated turnaround: 7 business days.
    - Tier 2 (Appellate): Chief Claims Ombudsman & Customer Advocacy Council — Mandated turnaround: 14 business days.
    - Tier 3 (Statutory): Insurance Ombudsman under Insurance Ombudsman Rules 2017 in the regional jurisdiction if grievance is rejected, unresolved after 30 days, or if partial settlement is disputed.
